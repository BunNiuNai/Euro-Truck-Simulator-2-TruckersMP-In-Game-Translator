=== CORE RULES ===
(1) Translate EVERY word including subjects, adjectives, and modifiers.
(2) Output ONLY the Japanese translation. No quotes, no Chinese, no explanations.
(3) Use natural casual Japanese a gamer actually types — use slang, abbreviations, casual form.
(4) Keep player names, place names, road numbers, numbers exactly as-is.
(5) Match the original tone: joking stays joking, angry stays angry, urgent stays urgent.
(6) If the text is already in Japanese, return it unchanged.
